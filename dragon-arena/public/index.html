    Controls

    WASD / arrows
    Move the selected dragon. If it is that dragon's turn the move happens at once; otherwise it is queued for its next turns. Orders straight into kelp or back into your own neck are refused unless you hold Ctrl (a deliberate sacrifice drops pearls for a team-mate).
    Shift + directions
    Shift + click
    Build a sprint: several steps in one turn. Every step after the first costs one segment. Release Shift to send it. Shift-click a tile — or an enemy head — to sprint there by the shortest open path (turn on Sprint range to see what is reachable).
    Q / E / X
    Split in half / split off a 2-segment child / click a segment to choose where to split. The rear part becomes a new dragon facing the other way.

    Rules in one minute

    • Each round, pearls tick, then every living dragon acts once, in ascending id order. A split's child gets the next id and acts later in the same round.
    • Eating a pearl grows you by one (the tail stays put that step). Pearls are the only way to grow.
    • You die by moving into kelp, any body segment (your own tail included — it has not moved yet), or another head. Head-on kills both dragons, whatever their size or team.
    • A dead dragon turns every second segment, starting at the head, into a pearl.
    • A dragon of length L can move up to L−1 tiles in one turn. That is what makes a cheap dragon a torpedo.
    • Portals come in pairs of the same orientation; you leave the partner edge still travelling the same way. The map wraps at its borders.
    • After the last round: longest living dragon wins, then greatest total length, otherwise a draw. A team with no dragons at the end of a round loses at once.
